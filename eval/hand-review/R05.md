## 3. Conflicts

1. p1 vs p1: "Any Wounds? No" but "Independent with Wound Care? No" is also ticked; the second should be n.a. Home health follow-up box blank.
2. p10 vs p14: H&P active inpatient list is four PRN medications (pain, anxiety, bowel, sleep) with no antipsychotic; the MAR shows olanzapine given repeatedly under three order lines (two daily doses and a bedtime dose) and no anxiolytic given. The H&P list is the admission snapshot, not the current regimen.
3. p10 vs p10: one exam line says "Follows directions. Cognitive functions intact." then "Unable to assess" due to mental impairment and uncooperativeness. Also "appears comfortable" vs exam limited by mental impairment.
4. p1 vs p9/p10: form presents a self-administering, non-cognitively-impaired patient whose only limitation is a diagnosis; H&P shows an involuntary hold for danger to others, psychotic features and an exam limited by mental impairment. Form "Self Administer All Meds: Yes" has no chart support.
5. p1 / p2 / p9 / p11: four labels for the same episode (Psychosis NOS; Bipolar Disorder, Unspecified; chief complaint DTO; bipolar with psychotic features). Not a true conflict, but an extractor may count them as separate conditions.
6. p1 vs p2 housing: "Currently Unhoused" vs filled (redacted) patient and guarantor address fields with a family member as primary contact. Cannot tell whether the address is current.
7. p1 vs p2 (not a conflict): ethnicity Hispanic/Latina vs race White are different fields.

## 4. Expected gaps

- Psychiatric progress notes: current mental status, SI/HI denial, hallucinations, stability on the current antipsychotic dose, whether the hold has lapsed or the patient is voluntary (MH-1, MH-2, MH-3).
- Un-redacted or current orientation statement (COG-2); nursing or behavioral notes on self-administration of medications and on behavior toward staff (COG-1, OTH-1).
- Nursing note or ADL assessment covering bathing, hygiene, toileting (ADL-1..3); ambulation distance or PT/OT note (MOB-2, MOB-3).
- Last five vitals sets with temperatures, within 24 hours (ID-1, DOC-6).
- MRSA and COVID swab results (INF-1, INF-2); EKG result (p9, p11); repeat BMP for the sodium of 135 (p11 "Recheck bmp").
- Reconciled discharge medication list and 30-day supply (DOC-7, DOC-10).
- Conservatorship/FSP status (MH-5); registered sex offender status (OTH-2).
- Authorization/LOA, requested length of stay, CalAIM referral date (DOC-3; p1, p3).
- Blank form items on p1: PPD/TB/CXR, home health, insulin and oral diabetes meds.
- Whether "too ill or frail" or re-hospitalization risk applies (GEN-1..3) and whether the p2 address is current.

## 5. Traps for an automated extractor

1. Redaction that looks like a value: p10 "Oriented x" and "AAO X" are each followed by a black box. Output must be redacted/cant_tell, never AOx4 or AOx0. Same for temperature, weight, BMI, BSA on p10 and temperature on p12.
2. Self-contradicting sentence: p10 neuro line says cognition intact and then unable to assess. A first-match extractor reports intact.
3. Form answer that should be n.a.: p1 "Independent with Wound Care? No" ticked although "Any Wounds? No". Do not manufacture a wound-care or home-health requirement.
4. Blank is not No on p1: PPD/TB, insulin, diabetes oral meds, home health, length of stay are blank; ticked "No" boxes (O2, infection, hospice, car) must not be read as blank.
5. Mixed tick styles on p1: some boxes carry small printed check marks, others large hand X marks that overrun the box ("Mental Health DX" X sits on the border near "Non-compliant"; the O2 and infection "No" X marks are half outside). p5 same: the X on "Recuperative Care" and on "homeless or at risk" is larger than the checkbox. Box-to-label assignment needs care.
6. Admission med list is not current meds: p10 H&P list lacks the antipsychotic; p14 MAR has it. Use the MAR for what was given.
7. MAR structure (p14): one drug appears as three order lines with different doses/frequencies; administrations are counted by "PERFORMED BY" rows; redaction bars cover the date/time column, clip the start of each row and cut the drug name of the last order. Do not sum doses or report three drugs.
8. Vitals counting (p12-13): one set at one time stamp is laid out as four DATE/TIME blocks (temperature, heart rate, respiratory, blood pressure) and the pain line spills onto p13. One set, not four or five. Date redacted, so "last 24 hours" is cant_tell. p13 is otherwise empty with grey bands.
9. Printed page numbers restart in each sub-document (CalAIM 1-6, H&P 1-3, flowsheet 1-2, EMAR 1 of 1). Cite packet pages, not printed ones.
10. Six nearly blank CalAIM pages (p3-8): only p5 has ticks. It is a referral request, not an authorization or LOA; a "CalOptima" header does not make DOC-3 met. Referral date blank; footer "Last Updated" redacted.
11. Hold code: p9 "placed on 5150m for DTO". 5150 is an involuntary psychiatric hold, not a dose, time or lab value; DTO is danger to others, not suicidality. Do not map 5150 to conservatorship (MH-5); do surface it for MH-1 and OTH-1.
12. Ordered is not resulted: "EKG ordered" on p9 and p11; no EKG in packet. "Recheck bmp" on p11 is pending.
13. Referenced is not included: p11 cites a psych nursing admission note as its data source; the note is not in the packet, so DOC-8 stays not_in_packet.
14. Lab in prose only: p11 sodium 135 labeled hyponatremia; no lab page. Record with page and quote; do not escalate.
15. Age is printed in text (p2, p9) while the "Age:" header field on p9-11 is blacked out; report it from where it is printed rather than "redacted".
16. Four diagnosis labels across p1, p2, p9, p11 are one episode; do not count them as separate conditions or as a conflict about the admission reason.
17. p1 ethnicity vs p2 race look contradictory but are different fields.
18. The provider's own printed footer on p1 (its address and form number) is template text, not patient PHI; a leak scanner should not flag it.
19. No sideways or upside-down pages here; orientation detection should return upright for all 14.
