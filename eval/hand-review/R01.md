## 5. Traps for an automated extractor

1. Three orientations: p11–24 rotated one way (text bottom-to-top), p25–30 rotated the other way (text top-to-bottom), p31 upside down with a diagonal "Missing Cosignature" watermark across the text.
2. CalOptima form pages are out of order (1, 2, 3, 6, 5, 4 at p3–p8); page footers, not position, give the sequence.
3. MAR grids run day-of-month columns 1–29 across the page with row labels and order text at the edge, often cut off; "X" cells mean not scheduled, not "given" or "refused"; "NA" is not a value. p25 begins "continued from previous page", so its pulse/resp/SpO2 rows belong to p24's night shift.
4. Behavior-monitoring sheets (p15, p17–20, p23) look like MARs but hold counts and code strings ("1,2,3", "1234", "2"); meal percentages (p19) and hours of sleep (p15) are not vitals or doses. Orthostatic lying/sitting/standing BPs on p11–12 must not be mixed with routine vitals on p24–25.
5. Only day-of-month is visible on flowsheets; month and year are redacted. "Last 24 hours" and "within 72 hours" questions must be cant_tell, never inferred from column position.
6. Drug names truncated at the page edge (p11 bedtime antidepressant, p15 and p20 antipsychotic, p16 and p18 second antidepressant, p21 hypnotic); PRN doses blacked out on p26–27 are redactions, not missing orders.
7. Blank checkboxes are not "No": p8 AOx4 all blank, p7 Mental Health DX all blank despite MDD on p2, p2 and p8 TB boxes blank despite PPD on p13–14, p2 "Attached Docs" none ticked although documents follow.
8. Near-empty pages are truncated notes, not blanks: p30 is an empty report ("No site of administration data found"), p32 is a header with no body, p33–34 are thin strips of a longer note.
9. The face sheet's key content is the diagnosis table on p10 (dementia, psychosis, homelessness); the gray header rows on p9 are unreadable and one ICD code on p10 is redacted. An extractor keyed on the referral form would miss the exclusion-relevant diagnoses.
10. p2 is an older provider form whose fields differ from the RC0725 checklist (e.g. "Attached Docs" list, "Continent of Bowel & Bladder"); map by meaning, not label.
11. "Resident", "Absent from facility", "Group Observed" wording on chart pages should not be rewritten as "hospital"; the sending setting is unknown.
12. PT note (p31): 97112/97116/97530 are billing codes, not measurements; "(I)" = independent, "DNT" = did not test, "300' x 2" is distance, "Good -" is a grade; "requires max encouragement" is not "max assist".
13. The POLST line "Long-Term Artificial Means of Nutrition" repeats on every MAR page; it is a code-status preference, not a tube feed (p8 says no tube feeding).
14. Stray values: a "2" on p23 in a letter-coded side-effect row; "36" in a miscellaneous face-sheet cell on p10 with no label.
15. Fax artifacts: handwriting on p1–p2 (page count, language, diagnosis, "NO" on p7); tick marks that cross box edges (p2 Female/Hospital Admit/Yes) can be read as the neighbouring box.
