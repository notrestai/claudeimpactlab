## 5. Traps for an automated extractor

1. No referral form. The only cover is a handwritten fax sheet (p1). Every FORM-* item must be not_in_packet; do not
   infer form answers from the chart.
2. Face sheet "Arrival Mode: Wheelchair" (p2) is not wheelchair dependence; PT shows 100 ft with a walker (p9).
3. Face sheet chief complaint and admission diagnosis (p2) are not the reason for the stay (p4, p6).
4. "Neur: Awake and alert" (p5, p14) says nothing about orientation; the orientation statements are elsewhere (p4, p6,
   p10, p13, p17). Never map "alert" to AOx4.
5. Copy-forward: p13 repeats the H&P text of p4 word for word; p17 repeats the p6 A/P and adds two lines. Dates are
   redacted, so recency cannot be proven; p17 is later only because it adds text. Do not double count or treat stale
   text as a second source.
6. Lab table (p14-15) spans two pages, column dates are redacted, and many cells are black boxes (CBC, potassium, BUN,
   calcium, magnesium, bilirubin, INR, BNP, HDL). Black cells are redacted, not missing; "--" and "< >" are not drawn or
   not displayed. Chronology of the six columns is unknown.
7. Diabetes false positive: hypoglycemia-protocol PRNs (dextrose, glucagon, glucose gel; p11, p16, p20-21) and a
   discontinued sliding-scale insulin order (p16). No diabetes diagnosis exists.
8. "D/C Medications (36h ago, onward)" (p6, p16) means discontinued orders, not discharge medications.
9. Urine culture value (p15) contradicted by two narrative lines (p16); surface the conflict rather than pick one.
10. "Pulse Min: 7" (p14) is a scan artifact, not bradycardia or a sepsis sign.
11. Vitals are 24-h min/max blocks plus one current value (p5, p14), not five discrete sets; temperature cells are
    redacted except one "(37 C)".
12. MAR (p18-21): date headers redacted; repeated 0900/2100 across columns are due slots, not administrations. Only the
    underlined "-Given" entries were given. Shaded "linked" rows on p20-21 are near-illegible hypoglycemia protocol.
